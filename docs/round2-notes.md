# Round 2 notes: request-led workflow (2026-11-27)

Labels: **F** verified fact, **S** published figure, **E** estimate, **J** judgment.

## Are the three asks right?
1. **RFP-style intake instead of NCT number: yes.** Feasibility is usually asked before a protocol is registered, so an NCT number rarely exists. The app now starts from a request form (pick-lists plus free text that auto-fills the fields). NCT lookup stays as "Trial lookup". [J]
2. **Automate the pillars: mostly yes.** Benchmark, country evaluation and scenario/risk are desk work and are automated here. Investigator insights cannot be fully automated: the answers must come from investigators. What can be automated is the survey design, the shortlist, sending, reminders and the analysis of answers, which is what was built. An expert should still check every output before it goes to a client. [J]
3. **More sources: yes, with care.** Adding registries widens coverage, but most extra registries have no open API (see below), and for observational studies registration is voluntary, so any registry count understates real activity. [J]

## What runs automatically now
| Pillar | Automated | Still needs a person |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Benchmark | Similar studies in the chosen countries (ClinicalTrials.gov), relevance tag, enrolment pace from completed comparable studies worldwide, ISRCTN, PubMed counts, curated published figures | Judging true comparability; curated figures exist only for the worked example |
| 2 Countries | Patient pool (GLOBOCAN × editable shares), research activity, experienced sites, competing studies, published papers, start-up time, weighted 0–100 score and tier | Start-up times and shares are judgment; check with local experts |
| 3 Investigators | Candidate sites and public study officials from registries; 5-pillar survey generated by rules from the request (plus up to 4 AI questions when Claude is on); invitation list, simulated sending, survey page, response dashboard, site ranking | Real contact details, sponsor approval to send, sponsor's own site lists |
| 4 Scenario & risk | Sample size for a proportion, three timeline scenarios month by month with country start-up and ramp-up, site allocation, rule-based risk register, verdict, printable proposal | Budget; final recommendation |

## Worked example: malnutrition in abdominal cancer surgery, Latin America (6 countries)
Run 2026-11-27 with live data. Treat as illustration; nothing here is expert-checked.
- 43 registry studies screened in the 6 countries, 19 related (8 close matches) [F count, J relevance]. Few registered precedents, mostly Brazil.
- Brazil scores highest (74/100): ~14,000 reachable surgical patients/yr [E], 13 related studies, 214 papers [F]; but the slowest start-up assumption (6 months) [J]. Mexico 49, Argentina 41; Chile, Colombia, Peru on the backup list with default weights.
- Expected malnutrition proportion 44% = median of four published figures (ELAN 50.2%, IBRANUTRI 48.1%, Santos 2022 GLIM 32.3%, nutritionDay 39.6% at risk) [S]. Sample 888 for ±4 points at region level with design effect 1.5 [E]; ±9.8 points per country if split evenly.
- Pace: 8 completed multi-centre observational studies give median 2.98 patients/site/month (P25–P75 1.71–6.59) [E]. With 30 sites: about 16 months base case including start-up; 25 months conservative [E]. Verdict: feasible with conditions.
- Main risks: different malnutrition tools across sites (fix GLIM as the definition), Brazil ethics timelines, selection bias (consecutive enrolment), country data-protection rules.

## Legal points for real survey sending (judgment, not legal advice)
- **Who may contact:** nothing found that stops a sponsor contacting investigators for feasibility; many sponsors use a CRO or a platform and blind their name until site selection. Offering Cohortia as the third party is a reasonable service model. [J]
- **Payment:** any honorarium for completing a survey should be fair market value and documented. US manufacturers report payments to physicians under Open Payments; similar transparency codes exist in other countries. [J; FMV and Open Payments are established practice]
- **Personal data:** investigator names and emails are personal data under GDPR, LGPD (Brazil) and similar laws; use a lawful basis, privacy notice, opt-out, business contact data only. [J]
- **Confidentiality and promotion:** share a synopsis, not the protocol; CDA before more detail; no product promotion in feasibility questions. [J]
- Confirm all of this with counsel before any real sending. Real sending is also outside the project rule on external activity until Sourov approves it.

## Known limits of round 2
- Relevance tagging is keyword-based; some unrelated studies are tagged related (e.g. a surgical-gloves neuropathy trial).
- Registries list few sites for observational studies; the site list (20 for the example) is thin and must be supplemented.
- Investigator names are study officials, not always the site PI; attribution to a site is heuristic.
- Start-up months, resection share (40%) and research-hospital share (25%) are judgment defaults.
- Patient pools exist only for cancers (GLOBOCAN); other indications need a prevalence entered by the user.
- Demo survey responses are synthetic and labelled as such.
